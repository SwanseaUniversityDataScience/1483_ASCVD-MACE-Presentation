## 📝 Project description

First presentation of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) is frequently with a fatal or non-fatal myocardial infarction of stroke. While effective management of modifiable risk factors (RF) reduces ASCVD risk, little is known about the quality of RF assessment and management preceding first presentation with major cardiovascular events. We aimed to evaluate documentation, treatment and control of cardiovascular RF before first-presentation MI or stroke in a population-wide cohort.
